# Escalation matrix and shift handover

## Matrix

| Trigger | First contact | Second contact | Third contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alarm in a plant room | platform on-call engineer | duty manager | director of engineering |
| Customer-visible loss | platform on-call engineer | incident commander | director of engineering |
| Access control fault | site security lead | platform on-call engineer | duty manager |
| Vendor on site without a ticket | shift supervisor | site security lead | vendor account owner |

The first contact is made by whoever observes the trigger. Nobody waits for permission to escalate; the
matrix exists to remove the question of whom to call, not to limit who may call.

## Handover

Each shift ends with a handover that covers three things: what is in flight, what is waiting on someone
else, and what looks wrong but has no ticket yet. The third item is the most valuable and the most often
omitted, so the handover template lists it first.

Handovers are written, not spoken. A spoken handover reaches the next shift and nobody else; a written one
reaches the engineer who is called at three in the morning two weeks later and needs to know why a circuit
was left isolated.

## Standing notes

The plant rooms are visited on a walking round twice per shift. Round times are recorded, because a round
that is consistently recorded at the same minute is a round that is being signed off from the office. The
security lead samples the timestamps monthly and has, on two occasions, asked for the round to be repeated
under observation.

Rooms under construction are excluded from the round only while the contractor's permit is open, and the
exclusion ends automatically when the permit expires. An exclusion that outlives its permit is treated as a
finding in the next audit.

## Out of hours

Outside working hours the estate is covered by a single engineer and a remote monitoring desk. Any isolation
of a circuit between 22:00 and 06:00 requires a second pair of eyes, either on site or on a video call with
the monitoring desk, and the name of the second person is recorded in the isolation log. The rule exists
because the two incidents in the estate's history that caused the most damage both involved a single
engineer working alone with a circuit they believed was dead.

## Review

The matrix is reviewed whenever a role is renamed or a contact changes. The review is a single task in the
operations backlog with a quarterly recurrence; it takes minutes and saves the kind of delay that only
becomes visible during an incident.
