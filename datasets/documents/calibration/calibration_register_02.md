## Notes

Entries are revised when an asset is re-measured by the facilities group, and a re-measurement that confirms
an existing entry is still entered as a revision. Superseded entries are struck rather than deleted, so a
ticket that quotes an older entry can still be reconciled against the schedule.

Identifiers are never reused. Where a replacement asset occupies the position of a retired one, the
replacement receives a new identifier and both appear in the schedule for one revision before the retired row
is struck.

Separators are transcribed by hand as often as they are copied. A line with the wrong number of separators is
rejected by the ticket validator before a work order is issued, which is the cheapest place for that defect
to be caught.
