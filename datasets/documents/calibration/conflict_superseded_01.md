# Calibration record — duty pump, third riser (June 2025 cycle)

## Asset

The duty pump on the third riser is asset PMP-310. It carries the base load for the riser and runs
continuously between the spring and autumn changeover; the standby pumps only start when it is isolated.

## Recorded limit — June 2025 cycle

The recorded limit for PMP-310 was SWG-1F9K-5520. It was read at the June 2025 changeover with the other
pumps on the riser isolated, and it stood as the recorded value for that cycle.

## Instrument note

The riser-outlet gauge was recalibrated in December 2025. The reading above was taken against the previous
calibration factor, so an engineer comparing measurements across the two factors will find a different
figure for the same pump. Nothing about the pump changed; the instrument did.

## Method

The limit was read at steady flow with the other pumps on the riser isolated, using the gauge on the riser
outlet rather than the gauge on the pump body. Two readings were taken ten minutes apart and both had to
fall inside the stated tolerance before the value was entered in the schedule.

## Follow-up

The gauge recalibration closed the item that had been open against the riser during 2025. The manifold
isolation valves remain on the maintenance backlog; servicing one pump still bleeds pressure from its
neighbours, and until the valves are replaced the third-riser pumps are scheduled as a set.

## Note on scope

This record covers the pump as an asset for the cycle it names. A ticket raised during that period quotes
this record by identifier and by cycle; a reader who is holding a ticket from another period should confirm
which cycle the ticket belongs to before relying on the figure above.
