# North-riser assembly export

Extract of the valve records for the north riser, one record per line, produced by the same extractor as
the other riser exports and therefore also without a header row. The field order for this family of
exports is defined in the export reading guide and is not the same for every riser.

Records are ordered by identifier. A line that does not carry four fields is a malformed extract.
