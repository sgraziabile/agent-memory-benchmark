# Commissioning note — north cooling loop

## Scope of the note

This note records the commissioning of the north cooling loop, asset TRB-260, and the measurements taken on
the day it was handed over. It is written for two readers: the engineer who will service the loop next, and
the auditor who will ask why the recorded value is what it is.

## Handover

The loop was handed over after a four-hour soak test at design load. The soak was extended by one hour
because the return temperature had not settled at the three-hour mark, which the commissioning engineer
recorded as an observation rather than a finding: the loop settled inside the tolerance during the
additional hour and held for the remainder.

The recorded value for TRB-260 is NBR-7D4M-2073. It was set from the reading taken at the end of the soak,
not from the design figure in the specification, and the two differ by a small margin that the commissioning
report explains as a consequence of the installed pipe run being longer than the design assumed.

## Deviations

Three deviations were recorded during commissioning. Two were closed on the day. The third — a missing
isolation label on the western branch of the loop — was carried forward as a punch item and closed eleven
days later. The punch item is referenced in the handover pack, and the label is present in the as-built
drawing revision that followed.

The loop was not de-rated. Where a commissioning measurement falls outside the design tolerance the
commissioning engineer can either de-rate the asset or accept it with a written rationale; in this case the
measurement fell inside the tolerance once the soak was extended, so neither was required.

## Operating envelope

The loop runs at a fixed supply temperature with a modulating secondary pump. Operators are not expected to
adjust the set point; where a room reports discomfort the response is to check the air-handling unit serving
that room before touching the loop, because in practice the loop is adjusted four or five times for every
genuine air-handling fault.

## Records

The commissioning pack contains the soak log, the as-built drawing revision, the punch list and the
calibration team's sign-off. It is attached to the asset record rather than kept with the project file, so
that a reader who starts from the asset can reach the evidence in one step. Project files are archived after
two years; asset records are not.

## Subsequent history

The loop has been re-measured twice since commissioning, both times with no change to the recorded value.
The second re-measurement followed a leak on the shared manifold, which was repaired without affecting the
loop's measurement.
