## Transfer notes

Two assets were transferred to Rosario from the Viaduct facility when the southern hall was consolidated.
Both kept their Viaduct identifiers and both were re-recorded here at the value they carried at transfer,
because the calibration team re-measures an asset after a move rather than assuming the value survives it.

A transferred asset that has not yet been re-measured is marked in the ticket system but not in this
register, which is a known gap: the register has no status column. The gap is recorded here so that a reader
who notices an unexpectedly early value knows why it looks that way.

## Scope

This document records values. It does not describe assets, and a reader who needs a description should use
the Rosario registry rather than infer one from an identifier. It also does not record inspection grades,
which live with the inspection reports and are revised on a different schedule.

## Custody

The register is maintained by the Rosario calibration team and reviewed when the Viaduct series is revised,
so that the two files do not drift in convention even though they are never merged. Where the two disagree
about a convention rather than a value, the disagreement is resolved by the facilities lead for the estate
and recorded in both revision histories.
